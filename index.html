About nyumbaFind

nyumbaFind is a simple community platform that helps people find vacant houses to rent across Nairobi, quickly and directly. Caretakers and landlords list vacant units — with photos, price, size and location — and house hunters can filter by price, size and area (constituency & ward) to find something that fits, then chat directly with the caretaker on WhatsApp to confirm availability.

To keep listings fresh, any upload that stays live for more than 3 weeks is automatically removed (assumed taken), and the site caps listings at 30 active uploads at a time to stay fast and manageable.
